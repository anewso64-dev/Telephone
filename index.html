Field notes

Every change is deliberate: there is no random dialogue to guess around. A useful line keeps the important words intact and lets one friend's interpretation feed the next.

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys. Press E near a friend, the clock shelf, cabinet, or exit. Press Q to switch between nearby targets. Movement pauses during conversations.
